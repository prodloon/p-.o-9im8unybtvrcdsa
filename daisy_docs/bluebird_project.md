# Bluebird Project Notes

The Bluebird project is our internal tool for tracking migrating birds across
the Pacific flyway. The lead coordinator is Dr. Helena Vasquez at the Monterey
field station.

## Funding

Bluebird is funded through September 2027 by the Cormorant Foundation grant
CF-2026-118, totaling $340,000. Renewal requires a published dataset by June.

## Telemetry hardware

Each tagged bird carries a 4.2-gram GPS logger sampling every 15 minutes.
Battery life is approximately 340 days at that rate. The firmware version
currently deployed is v2.3.1.
